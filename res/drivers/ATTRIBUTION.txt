PawnIO kernel-module blobs (AMDFamily17.bin, IntelMSR.bin, LpcIO.bin).

Source: LibreHardwareMonitor repository
(LibreHardwareMonitorLib/Resources/PawnIo/), Mozilla Public License 2.0,
Copyright (C) LibreHardwareMonitor and Contributors.
PawnIO driver itself: https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO

These are executed by the PawnIO kernel driver (installed once via
external/PawnIO/PawnIO_setup.exe) through our own client
(src/sensors/pawnio.rs) — no .NET runtime, no sidecar programs.
Only the modules needed by this machine's CPU are loaded at runtime.
